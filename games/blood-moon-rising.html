Blood Moon Rising

Horror Castle Crawl Card Game

👥 Players 2-4
⏱️ Time 60-120 min
🎯 Age 14+
🎲 Complexity Medium

As the Blood Moon rises, your band of brave souls must navigate the twisted passages of Dracula's palace—a place where sorrow is etched into the walls and each corner whispers of past atrocities. You will delve into the ancient, bloodied history of the undead lord, unearthing secrets steeped in malevolence and echoes of those who came before. The damned hold the key to ending Dracula's insatiable lust for dominion and vengeance for all time.

Mark the calendars for 2026, when the veil between worlds will thin, and the spine-chilling expedition begins. Will you rise to the occasion? Or will Dracula's sinister plot spiral the world into perpetual night? Will you be ready, when the Blood Moon Rises?

Key Features

  • ✓ Cooperative horror gameplay
  • ✓ Explore Dracula's twisted castle
  • ✓ Dark, atmospheric artwork
  • ✓ Strategic deck building
  • ✓ Immersive gothic horror theme
