How to Play

1

Take Turns

Players alternate placing one stone on any empty space on the 5x5 grid.

2

Form Rows

Create lines of your stones (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) while blocking your opponent.

3

Control Territory

Strategic placement creates multiple threats and limits opponent options.

4

Win

First to create a complete row of their stones wins. If the board fills, most stones in rows determines the winner.
