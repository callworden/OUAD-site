Prototype

Her Majesty's Airships

Steampunk strategy in the skies of Victorian England

👥 Players 2-4
⏱️ Time 30-40 min
🎯 Age 12+
🎲 Complexity Medium

Command your fleet of steam-powered airships in a race for dominance over Victorian London. Upgrade your vessels, outmaneuver rivals, and claim lucrative trade routes before your competitors.

Balance resource management with tactical positioning. Every turn presents tough choices: upgrade your flagship, expand your fleet, or race for control of premium sky-lanes?

Key Features

  • ✓ Engine-building meets area control
  • ✓ Gorgeous steampunk artwork
  • ✓ Asymmetric player powers
  • ✓ Multiple paths to victory
  • ✓ Scales great from 2-4 players

How to Play

1

Deploy Airships

Place your airships on the board, claiming positions on the London sky-map.

2

Collect Resources

Gather coal, parts, and prestige from controlled routes and completed contracts.

3

Upgrade & Expand

Enhance your airships with better engines, weapons, or cargo holds. Build new vessels to expand your reach.

4

Win Through Strategy

Victory points from trade routes, completed contracts, and fleet upgrades. Most prestigious captain wins!
