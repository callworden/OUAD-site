Blood Moon Rising

A cooperative horror castle crawl card game

👥 Players 2-4
⏱️ Time 45-60 min
🎯 Age 14+
🎲 Complexity Medium

The blood moon rises over Dracula's castle. You and your fellow hunters have infiltrated the ancient fortress, seeking to end the vampire lord's reign of terror once and for all.

But the castle is alive with dark magic, and time is running out. Work together, manage your resources, and survive the horrors that lurk in every shadow. One wrong move could doom you all.

Key Features

  • ✓ Cooperative gameplay with escalating tension
  • ✓ Modular castle layout - every game is different
  • ✓ Push-your-luck resource management
  • ✓ Gothic horror atmosphere with beautiful art
  • ✓ 30-minute setup, 45-60 minute playtime

How to Play

1

Setup the Castle

Draw random room cards to create the castle layout. Each room presents unique challenges and opportunities. Place your hunters at the entrance.

2

Explore & Survive

On your turn, move through rooms, fight monsters, gather resources, and avoid traps. Work together to plan your route and manage limited supplies.

3

Face Dracula

Reach Dracula's throne room before dawn breaks (tracked by the blood moon dial). The final confrontation requires teamwork and careful use of your gathered resources.

4

Win or Die Together

Defeat Dracula to win. If any hunter dies, or if dawn arrives before you reach the throne room, all players lose together.
