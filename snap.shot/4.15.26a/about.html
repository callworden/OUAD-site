Who We Are

We're Chris and Amy Allworden, a creative team based in Central Wisconsin, bringing games and stories to life through Once Upon A Dice (formerly Hyperbaby Games).

We believe play brings out the best in people. Creativity, imagination, and laughter combine to lift us up and brighten our days. That's why we actively encourage a playful attitude—even among adults—and why game development has become one of our most important projects.

At the end of the day, we're storytellers. Whether through interactive card games, choose-your-own-adventure books, or immersive narratives, we want you to not just consume our stories—but play them.

Amy lives for adventure, or at least mild action that doesn't require a lot of bending.
