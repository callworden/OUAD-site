Made by two people in Central Wisconsin

Once Upon A Dice is game designer Chris Allworden and fantasy author Amy Allworden. Every game starts with a world we want to spend time in. Then we fit it into a single deck you can learn in minutes and play for years. Bend the rules, make them yours, and bring friends.

Every game is free to play online. If one earns a spot on your table, the printed deck is how you keep it.
