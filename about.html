We Are Once Upon A Dice

Games with Creative Gameplay
and Immersive Stories

We believe play brings out the best in people. Creativity, imagination, and laughter combine to lift us up and brighten our days. That's why we actively encourage a playful attitude — even among adults. And that's what makes game development one of our most important projects.

Chris & Amy Allworden

Central Wisconsin

We've been developing games together for a while now — and we like to think that our love of playing is what drove us to take this madness one step farther. Our goal is to create games that are elegant, immersive, and accessible to all shapes and sizes of players.

At the end of the day, we are storytellers. We want you to enjoy our worlds — and even better, we want you to play them.

At Once Upon A Dice we bring our life experiences to everything we make. We know how much we appreciate the moments shared during life's journey, and we're excited to share with you the lesser-known paths around each corner.

Thank you for all your support and interest. Please come back soon — we're always into something new.

Chris Allworden Owner · Lead Designer · Illustrator
Amy Allworden Writer · Game Developer · Worldbuilder
