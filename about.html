Who We Are

We're Chris and Amy Allworden, a husband-wife creative team based in Central Wisconsin, bringing games and stories to life through Once Upon A Dice.

We believe play brings out the best in people. Creativity, imagination, and laughter combine to lift us up and brighten our days. That's why we actively encourage a playful attitude — even among adults — and why game development has become one of our most important projects.

Our love for playing drove us to take things one step further. Our goal is to create games that are elegant, immersive, and accessible to all shapes and sizes of players. At the end of the day, we're storytellers — and we want you to not just enjoy our stories, but play them.

Meet the Team

Chris & Amy Allworden

Founders of Once Upon A Dice

Chris is a digital illustrator and game designer who sees game design as the perfect platform to combine all of his passions into a physical form. He creates indie card games through Once Upon A Dice and provides graphic design services through Noble Pixel Studios.

Amy lives for adventure — or at least mild action that doesn't require a lot of bending. Growing up playing D&D, reading Forgotten Realms, and attending renaissance festivals, she brings a lifetime of fantasy experience to the table. With a BSc in Wildlife Biology and a year teaching English in Japan, she combines science, culture, and imagination into rich game narratives. Her debut novel Immolation kicks off the Raven Vs Crow series.
