Bounce Bunny Bounce

Radioactive Carrot Chaos! A game by Once Upon A Dice

Set up a game

Collect 3 Carrots first. Bounce your opponent off the field to slow them down.

Who are you playing?
Computer difficulty
How to play
  • Each turn, bounce your bunny one card up, down, left, or right. Bounce off an edge to wrap around to the other side.
  • Your bunny starts off the field and enters on any card in the bottom row.
  • The card you land on is revealed. Shock cards bounce you again toward their red stripe, and they can chain.
  • Stop ends your movement. Spy lets you peek at one hidden card. Card Swap trades two cards on the field. Bonus Shock gives back a Universal Shock (max 3).
  • Grabbing a Carrot ends your turn.
  • Land on the other bunny to bounce it off the field. Get caught in a Shock loop and you’re bounced off yourself.
  • After moving, you may spend a Universal Shock to bounce your opponent one card in any direction. They have to do whatever that card says.
